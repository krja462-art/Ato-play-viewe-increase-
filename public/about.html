About AtoPlay Booster

Version 2.4.0 • Android APK & PWA

What is AtoPlay Booster?

AtoPlay Booster is an organic community platform where AtoPlay video creators support each other. Creators watch each other's videos for 60 seconds, earn coins, and create promotions to gain genuine views, watch time, and channel reach.

Key Features

  • 60-second verified watch time tracking with token authentication.
  • Instant automated metadata extraction from AtoPlay video URLs.
  • Peer-to-peer reciprocal view exchange network.
  • Daily login streak bonuses and creator referral program.
  • PWA and Android APK compatibility.
