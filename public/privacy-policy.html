Privacy Policy

AtoPlay Booster • Last Updated: October 2026
Privacy Summary: AtoPlay Booster respects your privacy. We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the creator community showcase, AI Video Growth Assistant, and cloud wallet synchronization.

1. Introduction

AtoPlay Booster ("we", "our", or "the App") is an independent creator community and AI SEO toolkit designed to help video creators discover channels, generate viral video titles/tags/scripts, and share their content organically. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android APK, Progressive Web App (PWA), or web services.

2. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

We collect the following minimal user information:

  • Account Details: When you sign in using Google Authentication, we receive your public display name, email address, and profile picture avatar. We NEVER receive or store your Google password or any third-party platform password.
  • Creator Showcase URLs: Public video and channel URLs that you choose to share with the community.
  • AI Assistant & Voice Input (Microphone Permission): When using the optional Voice-to-Text button in the AI Video Growth Assistant, your browser's standard Web Speech API converts speech into text locally. We do NOT record, store, or transmit raw audio files.
  • Wallet & Community Activity Data: Virtual community coin balances, daily check-in streaks, and completed discovery sessions.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and synchronize your creator profile across devices.
  • Display your shared video campaigns to fellow creators in the community feed.
  • Generate AI-powered video titles, descriptions, SEO tags, and scripts based on your topic prompts.
  • Prevent automated spam or abuse of community resources.

4. Third-Party Services & Cloud Storage

We use industry-standard cloud infrastructure to protect and synchronize your data:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: To provide secure, single-click Google sign-in without handling passwords directly.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Encrypted NoSQL database used to maintain your creator profile, coin balance, and shared campaigns.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You may request permanent deletion of your account, email address, campaigns, and activity history at any time:

  • Directly within the App: Open the Menu Drawer > Legal & Support > Account & Data Deletion.
  • Via Email: Send an email with subject "Account Deletion Request" to our developer support team at krja462@gmail.com. All records will be permanently purged within 24 to 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is intended for content creators and is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at krja462@gmail.com.
