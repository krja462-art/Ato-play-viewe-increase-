Privacy Policy

AtoPlay Booster • Last Updated: October 2026
Privacy Summary: AtoPlay Booster respects your privacy. We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the peer-to-peer view exchange, coin wallet balance, and secure 60-second anti-cheat tracking.

1. Introduction

AtoPlay Booster ("we", "our", or "the App") is an independent creator community exchange application designed to help creators increase watch time, views, and reach on AtoPlay. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android APK, Progressive Web App (PWA), or web services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following minimal user information:

  • Account Details: When you sign in using Google Authentication, we receive your public display name, email address, and profile picture avatar. We NEVER receive or store your Google password.
  • Campaign & Video URLs: Public AtoPlay video URLs that you choose to submit for view promotions.
  • Wallet & Coins Data: Virtual coin balances, daily streak counts, referral rewards, and completed watch records.
  • Anti-Cheat Verification Tokens: Anonymous session tokens and timestamps to verify that creators watch videos for the required 60-second duration without tampering.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and save your coin wallet across sessions and devices.
  • Distribute and display your promoted AtoPlay videos to genuine creators.
  • Prevent automated bots, script abuse, and fraudulent coin farming.
  • Award streak bonuses, referral commissions, and promotional rewards.

4. Third-Party Services & Cloud Storage

We use industry-standard cloud infrastructure to protect and synchronize your data:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: To provide secure, single-click sign-in without handling passwords directly.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Encrypted NoSQL database used to maintain your coin balance, campaign status, and watch history.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You may request permanent deletion of your account, email address, campaigns, and coin balance at any time:

  • Directly within the App: Open the Menu Drawer > Settings > Legal > Account & Data Deletion.
  • Via Email: Send an email with subject "Account Deletion Request" to our developer contact at Developer. All records will be completely purged within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

7. Platform Disclaimer

AtoPlay Booster is an independent, community-driven view exchange and promotional tool. It is not affiliated with, authorized, maintained, sponsored, or endorsed by AtoPlay, Google LLC, or any of their affiliates.
